Real Project

36th Division NMC

Municipal Ward Complaint & Grievance Management App

A citizen complaint management system built for a municipal corporation ward, with a dedicated citizen-facing app and an admin app. Residents raise complaints — cleaning, electricity, public grievances, certificate requests, and other civic issues — the ward admin is notified, resolves the issue, and updates the status, while the resident is notified and can track progress in real time.

What we built

  • End-user app for raising complaints across multiple civic categories
  • Admin app for issue assignment, resolution & status updates
  • Real-time notifications for both residents and admins
  • Complaint status tracking from raised to resolved
